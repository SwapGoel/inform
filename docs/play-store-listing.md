Turn every phone unlock into two minutes of learning.

Inform quietly generates fresh, beautifully designed wallpaper cards covering:
• Current Events
• History

• Polity & Governance
• Economy & Society

Scroll an endless, never-repeating feed of short facts — tap any card to read the full source article. Play a new mind-game every day. Switch between English and Hindi anytime. No sign-up, no ads, no clutter — just enter a name (or skip it) and start.

Inform is designed to be light on your phone: no accumulating cache, no background bloat, and a feed that never runs out.
