# Privacy Policy for Inform

_Last updated: [DATE — fill in before publishing]_

This policy describes how the Inform app ("we", "the app") handles information when you use it.

## Information We Collect

The app does not collect or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no backend server that receives data from your device.

- **Display name (optional)**: If you enter a name during setup, it is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
- **Language preference**: Your chosen language (English/Hindi) is stored only on your device.
- **Content viewing history and game progress**: Stored only in the app's local storage on your device, purely so the app can avoid repeating content you've already seen and remember your daily game progress. This never leaves your device.
- **No account, no personal data collection**: The app does not require sign-up, does not collect your email, phone number, or contacts, and does not access your photos, location, or other device data.

## How the App Gets Its Content

The app periodically downloads publicly available content files (facts, headlines, source links) from a public content delivery network, so it can show fresh material. These are plain content downloads — no personal or device-identifying information is sent as part of them.

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because no data is collected from you in the first place. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs [update this line if you later add Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics/AdMob].

## Third-Party Content

The app links out to original news/government/reference sources for full articles, and may embed YouTube videos for educational content. Google's YouTube Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply when you play an embedded video: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data, including your name, language preference, and viewing history. Nothing about your usage is retained anywhere else, since nothing was ever transmitted off your device.

## Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children — indeed, it does not collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync or analytics). Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
